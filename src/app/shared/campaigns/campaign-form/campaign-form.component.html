Paid Collab — You pay the influencer a fixed fee to create and post content (Reel, Story, etc.). You review the submitted post and admin handles the payout. Best for awareness, launches, and product promotions.

Product Collab — You send a free product to the influencer in exchange for an honest review or post. No cash payment — the product itself is the compensation. Best for unboxing, reviews, and lifestyle integrations.

Invite to Location — You invite the influencer to visit your store, event, or venue for on-site content creation. Attendance itself completes the collaboration. Best for restaurants, launches, and experience-based brands.
