TrendStarz is a professional platform for creators, brands, and photographers to collaborate authentically. These guidelines help maintain a safe, respectful, and productive community for everyone. By using TrendStarz, you agree to follow these rules.

1. Real Profiles Only

  • Use your real name or registered business name.
  • Profile information must accurately represent you or your brand.
  • Fake, impersonation, or duplicate accounts may be removed without notice.

2. Complete Your Profile

A complete profile builds trust and improves your visibility on the platform. Please maintain:

  • Profile photo
  • Bio / About section
  • Location (State & District)
  • Social media links with accurate follower counts
  • Category / Niche

Incomplete profiles may receive lower visibility in brand and influencer searches.

3. Respect Other Members

TrendStarz is committed to a harassment-free environment. The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Verbal abuse or threats
  • Harassment or bullying of any kind
  • Hate speech based on religion, caste, gender, nationality, or sexual orientation
  • Discrimination against any member or group

Violations will result in immediate account suspension.

4. No Direct Payments Outside TrendStarz

For the protection of both influencers and brands:

  • Always accept campaign payments through the TrendStarz platform.
  • Avoid making or accepting payments directly from unknown members outside TrendStarz.
  • TrendStarz is not liable for disputes arising from off-platform transactions.

5. No Spam

The following activities are not permitted on TrendStarz:

  • Sharing affiliate links or MLM schemes
  • Posting unrelated promotions in campaign chats
  • Repeated unsolicited self-promotion
  • Sending bulk messages or connection requests for commercial purposes

6. Campaign Communication

All campaign-related discussions must take place through official channels:

  • Inside TrendStarz via the campaign messaging system
  • In campaign-specific WhatsApp groups created by TrendStarz (if applicable)

Avoid taking conversations to personal channels to ensure proper record-keeping and dispute resolution.

7. Report Suspicious Activity

If you encounter any of the following, report it to TrendStarz immediately:

  • Someone requesting money directly from you
  • Fake opportunities or fraudulent campaign offers
  • Impersonation of a brand, influencer, or TrendStarz staff
  • Any other suspicious behaviour

Report via the in-app report feature or email us at support@trendstarz.in.

8. Keep Contact Details Updated

To avoid missing campaign opportunities or payments, please keep the following up to date:

  • Email address (must be verified)
  • Mobile number (must be verified)
  • UPI ID or Bank account details

User Restrictions

Users may be restricted, suspended, or permanently removed from TrendStarz if any of the following are identified:

  • ❌ Email not verified
  • ❌ Mobile number not verified
  • ❌ Fake or inflated follower counts detected
  • ❌ Multiple duplicate accounts
  • ❌ Spam activity
  • ❌ Abuse or harassment of other members
  • ❌ Repeated campaign cancellations without valid reason
  • ❌ Fraudulent payment claims

Questions?

If you have any questions about these guidelines or need to report an issue, contact us at support@trendstarz.in.
