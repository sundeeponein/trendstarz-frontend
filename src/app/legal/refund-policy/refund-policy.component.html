1. Subscription Billing

Premium subscriptions on TrendStarz are billed according to the plan selected by the user. All payments are securely processed via trusted third-party payment gateways.

2. Refund Policy

  • Payments are non-refundable once a subscription is successfully activated.
  • Refunds are only considered in cases of duplicate charges or proven technical errors.
  • No partial or prorated refunds are provided for unused subscription periods.

3. Cancellation Policy

  • Users may cancel their subscription at any time to prevent future renewals.
  • Cancellation does not entitle the user to a refund for the current billing period.

4. Disputes & Support

For payment disputes or refund requests, please contact support@trendstarz.in. Our team will review your request and respond promptly.
